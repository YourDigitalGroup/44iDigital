Every white label partner has to pay for two things: running your campaigns and getting your sellers to sell them. Media margin can cover the first. We do neither. One license, $299 a month, covers everything that doesn't scale with spend:

  • Branded proposals, reporting dashboards, rate card, and collateral configured for your market.
  • Your Account Director, Trainer, and Campaign Manager — real people, direct lines.
