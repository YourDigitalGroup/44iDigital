What your AEs are selling

Every advertiser has typed their own business into Google and winced. SEO is the fix: site structure and speed, content built around what customers actually search, local listings and reviews, and the authority signals that move rankings. AEO extends the same work to AI search — structuring the site and its facts so ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews cite the business when customers ask questions.

It's the foundation tier of the foundation-up model: the work that makes every paid tactic cheaper and more effective, because the site it lands on finally converts.

Who it's for

Every local advertiser, genuinely — but especially service businesses that live on 'near me' searches: medical, legal, home services, auto repair. And any advertiser already spending on paid media over a weak foundation, which is exactly what a Fourge Insights audit exposes.

How it's reported

Rankings for the keywords that matter, organic traffic and conversions, listing accuracy, review growth, and AI-visibility checks — reported monthly under your brand, in plain language an AE can present.
