White label streaming TV & CTV advertising

Streaming TV advertising places your client's video ads inside the streaming services their customers actually watch — Hulu, YouTube TV, Prime Video, Netflix, and live sports — on the biggest screen in the house. 44i Digital builds and manages every campaign under your brand, so your AEs sell television's impact with digital's targeting and proof.

What your AEs are selling

Connected TV (CTV) is where local audiences went when they cut the cord — and it's the easiest digital conversation a broadcast or media seller can start, because the advertiser already believes in television. The difference is the buy: instead of a daypart and a demo, CTV is bought by location, by audience, or inside live sports, and every impression is accounted for.

Campaigns run across premium ad-supported inventory — Hulu, YouTube TV, Amazon Prime Video, and Netflix's ad tier — with frequency control across the household. Your client's ad plays full-screen, sound-on, unskippable in most placements.

Who it's for

Advertisers who want TV's brand impact with a local budget: auto dealers, healthcare, home services, retail, restaurants, and anyone sponsoring local sports. For broadcast partners it pairs naturally with spot — reach the cord-cutters your signal no longer covers, on the same screen.

How it's reported

Impressions, video completion rate, reach and frequency by audience, and — where the campaign supports it — offline visits: how many households that saw the ad later walked into the store. Reporting is monthly, branded to you, and written so an AE can walk a client through it without an ad-ops translator.

How it fits the program

Like every 44i Digital tactic, CTV recommendations come out of a Fourge Insights audit and follow the foundation-up model — the ad drives demand, and the advertiser's site, listings, and reviews are ready to catch it. Fulfillment is 100% US-based in Sioux Falls, priced wholesale on your rate card.
